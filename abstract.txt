RuFaS-gym: A Reinforcement Learning Environment for Whole-Farm Dairy Management in Idaho

Abstract

Idaho's dairy industry has consolidated into large confined operations along the Snake River Plain, bringing nitrate leaching into the Eastern Snake River Plain Aquifer, ammonia and methane emissions, and heavy irrigation demand. Because herd, feed, manure, and cropland form one coupled system, management decisions propagate to environmental outcomes across the farm and across years.

We present RuFaS-gym, a Gymnasium environment built on the Ruminant Farm Systems (RuFaS) whole-farm dairy simulator. It pauses RuFaS at each decision point, lets an agent observe the farm's state and set the next period's management, then resumes the simulation. An episode spans 84 monthly decisions over seven years, with a 54-dimensional observation and a 23-dimensional ration action. The environment leaves RuFaS's dynamics unchanged: stepped and unmodified runs agree on all 2,843 output variables.

On ration management, an optimized rule raises seven-year profit by 36% (USD 761,844) over the farm's configured ration, entirely by cutting feed cost 39% with no change in milk output; RL raises profit by [XX]% (USD [XXX,XXX]). We outline an expansion to fertilizer, manure, irrigation, tillage, herd, and crop decisions. RuFaS-gym provides a reproducible testbed for management strategies that balance dairy profitability with protection of Idaho's soil, water, and air.

Keywords: dairy systems, reinforcement learning, whole-farm simulation, nitrate leaching, greenhouse gas emissions, Idaho
